Spotlight 9 · Module 2 · Life & Living

My Home, My Rules!

Talk about household responsibilities, share your opinions and practise speaking English with confidence.

BEFORE YOU START

Record. Speak. Reflect.

Open a voice recorder on your phone or computer. Read each task carefully and record yourself answering the questions aloud. Then listen to your recording and complete the self-assessment checklist.

Focus on expressing your ideas clearly and using the vocabulary you’ve learnt.

01 / RECORD
Record your answers.
02 / LISTEN
Listen to yourself.
USEFUL LANGUAGE

Useful phrases

In my opinion, everyone should...

I think it’s fair / unfair to...

I’m responsible for...

I don’t mind doing...

I would prefer to...

We could take turns...

01 / WARM UP

Let’s Talk About Chores

Answer the questions aloud.

  1. What household chores do you usually do?
  2. Which chores do you enjoy doing?
  3. Which chores do you try to avoid?
  4. Should teenagers help with housework? Why?
02 / SPEAKING CHALLENGE

A Fair Share of Housework

Imagine that your family wants to create a new household chores schedule. Suggest a fair way to share responsibilities.

Speak for about 1–2 minutes. Explain who should do which chores, how often and why.

03 / SELF-ASSESSMENT

My Speaking Checklist

Listen to your recording and tick the statements that describe your performance.
