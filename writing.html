You’ve Got Mail!

Learn how to write an informal email, organise your ideas and check your writing skills.

01 / STUDY THE STRUCTURE

You’ve Got Mail!

Before you start writing, study the example below. Notice the greeting, opening, main body, closing and sign-off.

From: Sam   To: Alex   Subject: Household Chores
Greeting

Hi Alex,

Opening

Thanks for your email! It was great to hear from you.

Main body

In my family, we share household chores. I’m responsible for tidying my room and doing the washing-up. I don’t mind helping, but I can’t stand ironing!

What about you? Do you help around the house?

Closing

Write back soon,

Sign-off

Sam

02 / BEFORE YOU WRITE

Plan your email

Think about these questions:

  1. What household chores do you usually do?
  2. How often do you do them?
  3. Which chores do you enjoy or dislike doing?
  4. How does your family share responsibilities?
  5. What would you like to change?
Useful language: I’m responsible for... · I usually help with... · I don’t mind doing... · I can’t stand... · In my opinion... · I think it’s important to... · What about you?
