02 / WORD STRESS

Find the stressed syllable

Every English word has a stressed syllable. The stressed syllable is pronounced more clearly and strongly.

Remember! The symbol /ˈ/ shows which syllable is stressed.
AS-tronaut — /ˈæstrənɔːt/
en-VI-ron-ment — /ɪnˈvaɪrənmənt/

Read the words and find the stressed syllable.

  1. gravity
  2. resident
  3. exercise
  4. environment
  5. astronaut
  6. orbit
  7. giant
03 / PRONUNCIATION IN CONTEXT

Read aloud

Pay attention to word stress and sentence rhythm.

  1. Astronauts live on a space station.
  2. There is very little gravity in space.
  3. The space station is orbiting the Earth.
  4. The residents exercise every day.
  5. Living in space is a completely different environment.
Tip: Stress the important words. Keep unstressed words shorter and weaker.
